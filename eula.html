3. How units are used

  • A unit is used the first time a real migration (not a dry-run) runs for a given source, under your account and for the matching license type.

  • Dry-runs do not use units.
  • When you have used every unit you bought, the Software still opens, but starting a migration for a new source is blocked with a message that the license quantity has been used. You can buy more units at any time.
  • The Software shows your licenses, the quantity bought, the quantity used, the expiry dates, and the list of sources that have used a unit across all your projects. A used unit cannot be moved to another source.
