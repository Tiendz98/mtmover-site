Protect Microsoft 365 email on your own storage

MTMover backs up Microsoft 365 email from your own PC. Backup covers mailboxes only, not OneDrive, SharePoint or Teams. Backups are encrypted and incremental, so each run stores only what changed.

  • Encrypted backups
  • Incremental runs
  • Restore from the same app
  • Runs on your own PC

What it covers

Encrypted

Backup data is encrypted.

Incremental

After the first run, only new and changed items are added.

Restore

Restore backed-up email back into Microsoft 365.

How it works

Onboard

Connect the tenant with admin consent.

Backup

Select what to protect and start the backup.

Restore

Pick a backup and restore it when needed.

Free trial and limits

The free trial runs for 14 days and processes up to 5 items per module run, so you can test the full flow on real tenants. A license removes the trial item cap. See pricing.
