A practical migration sequence

01

Prepare

Inventory source mailboxes, confirm destination accounts and define a migration window. Record exceptions before any real job runs.

02

Map and dry-run

Connect the source and destination, map each source mailbox to its target, then use the trial workflow to validate access and configuration.

03

Migrate and verify

Run the real job, review the job result and verify representative content in the destination before closing the migration.

License model: Tenant-to-Tenant Email is $2 per source mailbox, with a $10 minimum order. Applicable taxes are calculated at checkout.

Pre-migration checklist

  • Confirm the exact source and destination mailbox mapping.
  • Verify that required accounts and permissions are ready.
  • Run a small validation set before the full batch.
  • Document items that require separate handling.
  • Keep the source available until destination verification is complete.
