Build evidence before production

01

Define the lab

Use non-production source and destination accounts. Record the starting item counts and the exact workflow you intend to evaluate.

02

Run the trial

Capture mapping, dry-run output, the real job result and any exceptions. Do not treat a successful login as migration evidence.

03

Compare outcomes

Verify representative content at the destination and document unsupported or separately handled items.

Production gate

  • Do not use a lab result as proof for an untested source system or workload.
  • Confirm permissions and rollback ownership before the migration window.
  • Protect screenshots and logs from tenant names, email addresses, tokens and customer data.
  • Keep a separate record of limitations and manual follow-up work.
