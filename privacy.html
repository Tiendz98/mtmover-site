Privacy Policy

What MTMover stores, why, and what it does not.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data MTMover collects when you use this website, the MTMover desktop app and the MTMover license service, why we collect it, and what choices you have. The controller of this data is MTMover, operated from Hanoi, Vietnam. You can reach us at contact@mtmover.tech.

1. The short version

  • MTMover runs on your own PC. Mailbox, file and Teams content that you migrate or back up moves between Microsoft 365 (or your IMAP server) and your PC. It is not sent to MTMover servers.
  • Our license service stores only what is needed to run accounts and licenses: your email, a salted hash of your password, your licenses, and which source items have used a license.
  • We do not sell personal data and we do not run advertising.

2. Data we collect

DataWhy we need it
Account: email address, a salted hash of your password (never the password), whether the email is verifiedTo create your account, sign you in to the app and the website, and send account emails.
Verification codes: 6-digit codes for registration and password reset, stored as hashes and valid for a short timeTo confirm that you own the email address.
Computer: an identifier of each computer you activate, and its nameTo apply the trial and to limit the number of computers per license.
Licenses: license type, quantity, purchase and expiry dates, and where the license came from (for example, an online order or a manual grant)To activate your licenses and show them in the app.
License usage: for each licensed migration, the product, an identifier of the source item that used a license (a mailbox address, a OneDrive account or a SharePoint or Teams site address), a project identifier and the timeTo count licenses, to stop at the purchased quantity, and to show you which sources you have already migrated. We receive only these identifiers, never the content.
Orders: handled by our payment provider (see section 4). We receive your name or company, email, country, order number, products, amount and payment status. We do not receive your full card number.To deliver your license, answer support requests and meet tax and accounting duties.
Support email: the messages and attachments you send to usTo answer you.
Website logs: standard technical data such as IP address and browser type, kept by our hosting providerTo deliver the site and protect it against abuse.

The source identifiers above can include email addresses of your own users. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to share them with us. We use them only for license counting and for showing them back to you.

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies of its own. A payment page run by our payment provider may set the cookies it needs to process a payment. When you sign in on mtmover.tech/account, your browser keeps a sign-in token in its local storage until you sign out or it expires after 30 days.

3. What we do not collect

We do not receive the content of your mailboxes, files, chats or backups. We do not read your Microsoft 365 data on our servers.

4. Who we share data with

We use a small number of service providers. Each one processes data only to provide its service to us.

  • Cloudflare hosts the MTMover license service and its database.
  • Resend delivers the verification, reset and license emails we send from noreply@mtmover.tech.
  • Paddle sells our licenses as the merchant of record. Paddle processes the payment, collects and pays sales tax and VAT, and handles refunds. Paddle acts as an independent controller of the payment data it collects, under its own privacy policy.
  • GitHub hosts this website and the installer download.
  • Microsoft hosts the mailboxes we use for support email.

These providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. We may also disclose data when the law requires it.

5. How long we keep data

We keep account, license and usage data while your account exists, so that your licenses keep working and you can see your migration history. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete it, except records we must keep for legal, tax or fraud-prevention reasons. Order records are also kept by Paddle as required by law.

6. Security

Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Traffic between the app, the website and the license service uses HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict some processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. Write to contact@mtmover.tech from the email address of your account. We aim to answer within 30 days.

8. Children

MTMover is a business tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. For material changes we will also notify account holders by email or in the app.
