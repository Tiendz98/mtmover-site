Refund Policy

When and how you can get a refund.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

We want you to try MTMover before you buy: the free trial runs all modules for 14 days. If you still buy a license and it does not work out, this policy explains your options. Online purchases are sold by our payment provider Paddle, the merchant of record, so refunds are paid back through Paddle.

1. 7-day refund

You can ask for a full refund within 7 days of your purchase if you have not used what you bought:

  • Migration licenses (Email, OneDrive/SharePoint/Teams sites, Teams chat, IMAP): refund for units that have not been used by a real migration. Units that have been used are not refundable, as the service has already been delivered.
  • Mailbox Tools and Backup & Restore (1 year): refund if no real job has been run with the license (for Backup & Restore, no real backup).

2. If the software does not work

If MTMover fails to work as described and we cannot fix the problem in a reasonable time after you report it, you may also ask for a refund of the affected units, even after the 7 days or after use. Please send us the details (see the Support page) so we can try to fix it first.

3. How to ask for a refund

  1. Email contact@mtmover.tech from the email address of your MTMover account.
  2. Include your order number or the email used at checkout, and a short reason.

We reply within a few business days. You can also contact Paddle directly through the link in your receipt. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method. The time the money takes to appear depends on your bank or card issuer.

4. What happens to the license

When a purchase is refunded, the refunded units and licenses are removed from your account.

5. Custom and invoiced orders

For orders arranged by email with a custom price or invoice, the terms agreed in that order apply. If none are stated, this policy applies.

6. Chargebacks

Please contact us before you start a chargeback with your bank, so we can solve the problem faster.

7. Your legal rights

This policy does not limit any refund or withdrawal rights that you have under the law where you live.
