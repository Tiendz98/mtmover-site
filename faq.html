Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions we hear most.

What is MTMover?

A Windows desktop app for Microsoft 365: tenant-to-tenant migration (Email, OneDrive, SharePoint, Teams), IMAP migration, mailbox tools, and encrypted backup and restore.

Where does the app run, and where does my data go?

MTMover runs on your own PC and talks to Microsoft 365 through Microsoft Graph. Your mailbox and file content is not sent to MTMover servers. The MTMover license server only holds account and license data. See the Privacy Policy.

Does MTMover change the source tenant?

No. MTMover only copies. The source gets read-only access.

What does the free trial include?

All four modules for 14 days, with up to 5 items per module run.

Can I run a migration more than once?

Yes. Re-run as often as you like. Items that already arrived are skipped, so deltas do not create duplicates.

What is dry-run?

A run that shows what would be copied without writing anything. It is on by default.

Which permissions do I need?

A Global Administrator of each tenant to grant admin consent during Onboard.

How do I reset my password?

Use Forgot Password? in the app, or open the account page. We send a 6-digit code to your email.

How do I create an account?

Use Create account in the app, or open the account page. You confirm your email with a 6-digit code.

How many computers can I use?

A license can be activated on a limited number of computers. For more, or to move a license, write to contact@mtmover.tech.

What does not migrate perfectly?

SharePoint pages: standard web parts are copied, but News, Events and People web parts, gradient section backgrounds and banner title text must be rebuilt by hand. Hyperlink column values keep their original URL.

Still have a question?

Write to us and we reply by email.
