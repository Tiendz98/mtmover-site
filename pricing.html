Pricing

Per-unit licenses. Try everything free for 14 days first.

Simple, per-unit pricing

Pay only for what you migrate. Dry-runs are free. Migration licenses never expire. Backup & Restore and Mailbox Tools last one year. Minimum order is $10.

A license belongs to your MTMover account email. Create an account or sign in first, then buy with the same email. Use the cart button at the top right to review your order and check out.

What counts as a unit?

LicenseOne unit is
Tenant to Tenant: Emailone source mailbox
Tenant to Tenant: OneDrive / SharePoint / Teamsone site: one OneDrive account, one SharePoint site or one Teams site
Tenant to Tenant: Teams chatone source user
IMAP Migrationone source mailbox
Backup & Restore (email only)one mailbox, per year
Mailbox Toolsthe full option, all features, per year

A source uses one unit the first time a real job runs for it. Running it again, or moving it to another destination, does not use another unit. When all units are used, new sources are blocked until you buy more. Prices are in US dollars; taxes are added at checkout where they apply. See your licenses and the sources already used on the account page.
